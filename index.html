About

Research with an engineering path to adoption

I am an AI researcher and engineer at the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence. Before BAAI, I worked at ByteDance and Meituan. My work has evolved from computer vision and OCR to domain language models, post-training, AI agents, retrieval, and multimodal reasoning.

I aim to release the code, models, datasets, evaluation assets, and training recipes behind my research whenever possible.

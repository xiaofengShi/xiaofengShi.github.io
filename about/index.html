Biography

Research shaped by building real systems.

I am Xiaofeng Shi, an AI researcher and engineer at the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI). I previously worked at ByteDance and Meituan.

我的研究关注如何让大模型真正进入专业领域，并尽可能开放论文对应的代码、模型、数据与评测资源。
